Project

ConvertToSonus is a video-to-sonification project that transforms scientific visualizations into sound. By analyzing pixel-level information and color variations in NASA's scientific visualization videos, our engine translates visual patterns into auditory representations.

Using the CIELAB color space and audio mapping techniques, ConvertToSonus connects visual characteristics with sound parameters such as pitch and intensity. This allows changes in scientific visualizations to be experienced through sound rather than sight alone.

Our project explores a different dimension of scientific communication, creating an alternative way to interact with environmental information and making scientific visualizations more accessible to different audiences.

Because we believe science should be experienced in more than one way for everyone. That's why ConvertToSonus is more than just a website.

We make you hear what you can not see.
