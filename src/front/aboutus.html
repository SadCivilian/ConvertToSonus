About Us

Meet the team driven by curiosity, collaboration, and innovation.

Our Vision

To create a new way of experiencing our planet by making Earth’s complex environmental data accessible, interactive, and meaningful through sound. We envision a world where scientific information can be explored not only through raw numbers and images but also through the universal language of music.



Our Mission

Our mission is to transform NASA's Earth observation data into meaningful sound experiences through data sonification. By combining scientific information with technology and creativity, we aim to make environmental changes easier to perceive, understand, and explore.


Our Goals

  1. Make Science Accessible: Present complex environmental data in a simple and engaging way where everyone can interact with it.
  2. Encourage Exploration: Allow people to discover patterns through interactive sound experiences.
  3. Promote Scientific Curiosity: Allow and inspire other people to explore NASA's open data and learn more about our planet.
